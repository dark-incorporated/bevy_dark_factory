## Found while fixing

- **The release's scripting timers never fired**: `bevy_society` turns on `rhai`'s `f32_float`, a `family_all` build
  unifies it into the editor's scripting, and `timer(name, seconds)` was registered for `f64` (#687).
- **`kill -KILL -<pgid>` could signal every process of the user** on procps-ng 4.0.4: a pgid starting with 1 became
  `kill(-1, …)` (#697, now `rustix::process::kill_process_group`).
- **The review's "cheap switch after the profile toggle" was a refused switch** (#678): `Cmd+F`/`Cmd+E` rows were
  Shift-indifferent.
- **#663 was not Tap**: every measured Tap also sent `modifiers`, a field bevy_fairy 0.3.1 dropped without a word.
  The pin bump to 0.13 then broke the editor's tour (fairy 0.12 moved checkpoint evaluation into `DebuggerPlugin`,
  which a headless editor does not add); reverted on the integration branch, fixed upstream as bevy_fairy#19.
- **frame_ms equalled busy_ms by construction** (#677): two clocks with the same bounds. Settled frames are now
  12 ms of the editor's own work in a 49 ms frame (p50, big, load ~20).
- **Kit's arrival re-measured every unlisted `.glb`** (225–681 ms per switch on big) and re-decoded 90 palette PNGs;
  `StateTransition` on a Kit arrival went from 142–356 ms to 30–40 ms (#678). Switches are still over 100 ms busy.
- **The stepped-clock reload test assumed three frames beat a 100 ms wall-clock debounce**; under `family_all` they
  do not. The test now holds the index's sweep while the clock steps.

## Process lessons (measured)

- **Worktrees sharing one `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` run each other's binaries.** Cargo names workspace members by their
  workspace-relative path, so two worktrees produce the same `editor-<hash>`; four workers reported passes or failures
  from a peer's build. A per-worktree `--config profile.dev.package.bevy_dark_factory.codegen-units=<N>` gives a tree
  its own hash without a second target dir.
- **`incremental = true` on main (rust-speed, this morning) filled two disks within hours**: 33 GB of incremental
  cache on bmb and 10 GB on mac_air from worker builds that did not export `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0`.
- **One editor rustc is ~15 GB**: eight workers compiling on big at once left 3 GB free under the gate. A
  `big-worker.lock` serialised them, which then queued workers 60+ minutes; moving test runs to bmb and big_mac was
  what kept the wave moving.
- **Wall-clock budget tests fail at load 50–90** (`opening_a_project_never_holds_a_frame_past_the_budget`,
  `family_settings_reload`): the hook's rerun-alone path (#580) is what separates them from real failures.
