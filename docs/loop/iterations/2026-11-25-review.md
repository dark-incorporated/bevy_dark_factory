## Item 8 — where the UI can get out of the way

**Art share per workspace**: MAP **32.30%** at 1600×1000 and **34.85%** at 1100×700. **The docks do not shrink with
the window** — the left dock goes 426 → 302 px and gives the viewport exactly what it gave up, so **a smaller screen

## Build and delivery

- **#671 — one directory rescan is 43% of every atomic write.** `atomic_file::put` calls `sweep_stale` on each
  `Flush::Full` write; `sweep_stale` does `fs::read_dir(parent)` over the whole directory. Measured 7.66 of
  17.75 ms per write, and the slowest test's 174.6 s is 10,200 of them. **And `atomic_file::write` is always
  `Flush::Full`, so every editor save pays it** — a latency bug as well as a test cost.
- **#672 — six slow tests hold independent cases in one serial test**: 27–37 fresh editors in two, 16,752 frame
  updates in three. ~220 s of the run half removable without dropping an assertion.
- **#673 — the gate's memos are per worktree**: 1 of 98 worktrees has them, so **17 trees re-ran the suite, 5.0 h**.
- **#674 — CI containers have no memory limit** on a zram host and cannot see the gate's lock: **13 of 14 reds are
  not the code**.
- **#675 — the gate times 10 checks not at all**; `changelog.py check` alone spawns **481** subprocesses. And the
  new `gate-tests` logs answer #625: **29% compile, 71% run**.
- **Duplication confirmed**: `ci.yml:90,92` are character-for-character the gate's two commands, on a tree the gate
  already passed.
