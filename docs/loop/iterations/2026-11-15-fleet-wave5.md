## What went wrong, measured

- **big's memory, not the code.** big's RAM is held by its owner's services (hs-distill 5.3 GB, vllm, llama-swap, 16 GB of `/tmp` tmpfs) and swap sat at 26 of 32 GB. systemd-oomd killed the 0.6.8 gate twice (at 30 min and at 6.5 min): a user unit's `ManagedOOMPreference=omit` is not honoured (the unit reported `none`), so the setting protects nothing. It passed on the third run with `MemorySwapMax=0`, `MemoryMax=13G` and `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2` (1430 s). The manager then waited for an hour on a unit that was already dead: a wait on a gate must also test the unit's state, not only the log.
- **The same cause turned two CI workflows red.** bevy_gooey's CI linked 37 examples in parallel and `ld` was killed (`signal 9`) in 11 of 12 runs; one run was a real flaky test (`each_surface_casts_its_heights_stack_from_the_skin` read the wall clock). The scheduled CI of this repo was killed mid-nextest on three nights. Fixes: `cargo check --examples` and a pinned frame time in gooey; `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=3`, `NEXTEST_TEST_THREADS=2` and a timeout on this repo's job. The manager did not look at the red CI after pushing gooey main; it should have.
- **The Releases page showed 0.6.5 while 0.6.6 and 0.6.7 existed.** 0.6.6's macOS build failed (a rhai `f32`/`f64` mismatch); 0.6.7's push run failed at signing (disk full) and its manual re-run built and notarized but did not publish, because `workflow_dispatch` only publishes with `publish=true`. A release is cut by pushing the tag.
- **Baselines are expensive.** Any change under `crates/`, even a crate README, forces a retake of the baseline (about $23 and 35 minutes on big), and the `release: X` commit has to be cherry-picked directly above the report commit.
- **Credentials.** bmb's Anthropic login expired during the wave; the rotating OAuth token on mac_air must not be borrowed. #416 (Claude Code beside omp) therefore waits for a human login.
- **Workers wrote outside their lane.** One deleted a shared scratch directory's `*.sh`; one reload of `llama-swap.yaml` stops olmocr for about a minute. Both are in the DM-01 notes.
